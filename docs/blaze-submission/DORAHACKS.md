# Cashingularity | Non-custodial BCH batch payouts

Pay contributors, contractors, and bounty recipients in one Bitcoin Cash transaction. Paste a list or import a spreadsheet in **Wallet → Batch Pay**, then review each destination, BCH amount, and miner fee before approving in your wallet. Wallet also supports individual transfers and WizardConnect. Markets offers BCH/USD charts and Paper Trading. Arcade hosts Rocket Race.
